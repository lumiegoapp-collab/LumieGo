Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LumieGo ("we", "us", or "the app") handles your information. We built LumieGo to collect as little data as possible. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information (Sign in with Apple)

When you create an account using Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, only on your first sign-in, the name and email address you choose to share. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, we only ever see Apple's private relay address. This information is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) and is used solely to maintain your account.

Camera, microphone, and photos

LumieGo needs access to your camera and microphone to record video, and to your photo library to save the videos you create. Your recordings are processed on your device and saved to your device (Photos and/or Files). We do not upload, view, or store your videos, photos, or audio on our servers.

Purchases

If you subscribe to LumieGo Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide the app's features (recording, teleprompter, saving to your device).
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Where your data is stored

Account information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, under appropriate security controls. Your media never leaves your device through LumieGo.

4. Data sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the app (such as Apple for sign-in and payments, and Supabase for account storage), and only as needed to provide the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). When you do, your account record is permanently removed from our database. You may also email us to request deletion.

6. Children

LumieGo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.

9. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email us at lumiego.app@gmail.com.
